# 0.11.0-claudegpt.131

## Reframe's messages wait while you write in a thread

- A message another thread sent (wake_thread), a scheduled task's run, a monitor's note or a report was typed into the
  thread's prompt even while you were writing in it, and Enter sent your half-written message with it.
- Each thread has a queue for Reframe's own messages now. You own the prompt from your first key until you send it
  (Enter), clear it (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+U, or deleting what you wrote), and for 3 seconds after any key. Messages wait in
  the queue meanwhile and go in one at a time afterwards; keys you type while one goes in follow it.
- What you wrote is followed from your keys (the prompt is not read). After the history's arrows or Ctrl+W the prompt
  stays yours until you send or clear it. A thread that starts again has an empty prompt.
- Monitor warnings' "Notify thread" shows "Waits for your prompt" while the note waits. Waiting is logged in main.log.
