# ClaudeGPT Reframe 0.11.0-claudegpt.69

- **Threads another thread opened are listed as its subagents.** In the session list (projects and recents), a thread opened with `open_thread` sits right under the thread that opened it, indented, instead of among the others (for example the Skyrim workers under Skyrim). The parent shows a `▾ n` / `▸ n` count: click it to hide or show its subagents. By default they are shown while any of them is not ended and hidden once all have. Threads opened before .67 have no recorded opener and stay where they are.
